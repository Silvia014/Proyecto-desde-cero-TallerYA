Frequently asked questions

Where do workshops take place?

Workshops take place in person in Bogotá. See the workshop listing for the venue.

When can I cancel?

You can cancel up to 48 hours before the workshop starts.

Where can I download my certificate?

Sign in to Learn TallerYA and select the workshop you completed. If the PDF is available, you can download it from your account.
